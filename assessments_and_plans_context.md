**Background:**
"assessments_and_plans_specs.md" outlines a new backend data standard for adult social care needs assessments and care plans in England. Under the Care Act (2014), local authorities must assess adults across 10 specific outcomes; if the adult is eligible, the authority must provide a care plan and personal budget. Currently, local authorities use highly heterogeneous systems and forms (from various CMS platforms to simple Word documents).

Because local authorities prefer their unique front-end designs, this project does not enforce a single national form. Instead, it standardises the *back-end* information to enable interoperability and data sharing between agencies.
