Our website is currently under development and we will be iterating it over the coming months. This page outlines current plans for website content. 

**Website objectives**

Our website aims to:

* **Engage the sector:** Explain the programme, its purpose, benefits and progress, and provide clear routes for audiences to ask questions, contribute and stay involved. Act as a vehicle to support collaboration between the programme team, different government departments, local authorities, vendors.
* **Repository for the standards:** Act as the single source of truth for the latest standards, technical documentation, records of decisions/rationale and supporting materials.
* **Explain the standards:** Provide plain English content that explains why open standards matter, how standards are developed and governed, and what they mean for different users.
* **Support implementation:** Give technical implementers and vendors, data providers, commissioners and local authorities practical guidance on how to adopt, test and use the standards in practice.
* **Provide latest information on programme activity:** Provide updates on pilots, implementation learning, working groups and future development so users can understand what is current, what is changing and how decisions are made. Long term, this will be replaced with latest ‘standards body’ activity and explanation of how standards are maintained by the body.

**Website roadmap**

We have split our website development into three phases. 

**Phase 1 (Complete)**: Develop an MVP website outlining the programme, our latest standards, and providing information on how to get involved.

**Phase 2 (Current)**

_Goal:_ The website is sufficient to provide an introduction to the programme for new audiences and can be shared with new stakeholders e.g. potential pilot sites. The website provides key information to support pilots (including relevant use cases and implementation guidance) alongside insight into the impact of our pilots.

Our backlog of future content for this phase includes:

* Our theory of change
* Pilot narrative
* Updated use cases covering Families First and Neighbourhood Health
* Additional implementation guidance to support pilots
* Validator tool
* Further impact stories or case studies of where we are piloting
* Research repository of our previous research to support use case development
* Alongside this, we will continue to provide ongoing updates to key content including adding Working Group minutes and the latest standards documentation.

**Phase 3 (Future)**

_Goal:_ Website becomes the long-term home for our standards, guidance on how to implement them, a route to provide ongoing feedback, and information on how they are maintained.

Our backlog of future content for this includes:

* Updated implementation guidance
* How standards are maintained
* Alongside this, we will continue to provide ongoing updates to key content including the latest standards documentation.

**Future Content and Website Feedback**

If you have any feedback on the website or additional content you would like to see, please reach out via [email](mailto:DataStandards-SocialCare@socialfinance.org.uk) with any comments.
