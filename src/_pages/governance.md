This section explains how the programme is governed and how we work with related programmes across government. It covers the role of the Steering Group and the programmes we work with to keep our standards aligned.

## [Steering Group](/steering_group)

The Steering Group provides strategic oversight and technical leadership for the programme. It provides advice to the Department for Education (DfE) and Department for Health & Social Care (DHSC) on the programme's scope, strategic direction and priorities. Read more about its responsibilities and membership on the [Steering Group](/steering_group) page.

## [Related Programmes](/related_programmes)

Our work is relevant to a number of other programmes across government. We monitor and actively engage with these programmes to ensure alignment and to keep them informed about our work. See who we are working with, and who to contact, on the [Related Programmes](/related_programmes) page.
