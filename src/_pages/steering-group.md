The Programme is governed by a Steering Group, which meets quarterly to provide technical leadership to the programme. The Steering Group's primary purpose is to provide advice to the Department for Education (DfE) and Department for Health & Social Care (DHSC) programme stakeholders.

## Steering Group Responsibilities

* **Strategic direction:** Advising on the overall vision and strategic direction of the data standard programme, navigating the goals of stakeholder organisations, including DfE, DHSC, LAs, and current/potential technology vendors.
* **Stakeholder management:** Ensuring the programme considers the needs of all key stakeholder groups, and ensuring effective communication and engagement is in place.
* **Overseeing the development of the standards:** Reviewing the work of the standards working group(s) and reference group(s), and the quality of their outputs.
* **Decision-making:** Providing expert advice to DfE and DHSC, regarding programme scope, strategic direction, and timelines, escalating issues as necessary. Guide the development and use of standards - for example, deciding which features to implement based on feedback from the standards community.
* **Risk management:** Reviewing and approving the programme's risk management strategy and ensuring risks are recorded and appropriate mitigation measures are in place.
* **Change management:** Providing advice to DfE and DHSC on any significant changes to the programme scope, budget, or timeline.
* **Issue resolution:** Facilitating the resolution of any escalated issues or conflicts related to the programme.
