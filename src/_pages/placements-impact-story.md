We're now moving beyond testing the standards in principle to piloting them in the real world, learning what it takes to embed them into real operational systems and workflows.

These pilots enable us to:

* Deliver early impact with our standards
* Learn quickly about how our standards work and what we might need to change to ensure they deliver benefits for the people who need to use them
* Learn about standards implementation and what support / guidance is needed to support high quality implementation
* Identify what enables sustainable governance
* Build the profile of our standards

This year we started our pilots with our Placements Standard. 

## Placements pilots: Our impact
We’ve now completed implementation of our Placements standard in two regions, with work underway in two more, expected to finish in October 2026. 

We’ve developed several key tools as a result of this work:

* **An implementation playbook:** A key outcome of this work has been the development of an implementation playbook. It captures the practical steps, decisions and lessons learned from early adopters, so future regions and local authorities can implement the standards more quickly and consistently. We're also learning more about how the standards can be embedded directly into case management systems -- important because standards are most valuable when they become part of day-to-day recording, rather than something that sits alongside existing processes.
* **A maturity assessment model:** Not all implementations are equal. Some areas may have adopted the right fields, while others have embedded the standards more fully into workflows, reporting and decision-making. We're developing a maturity assessment model to help local authorities, suppliers and regional partners understand where they are on that journey - and what further support they may need. 

## What’s next for Placements?
Our ambition is for all regions to be using the Placements standards by mid-2027, supported through the Regional Care Cooperative programme. We're also beginning to explore adjacent use cases, where the same standards could support wider planning, commissioning and market-shaping activity. 

## Future pilots
Beyond our Placements standard, we’re now exploring how we can pilot our other standards over the coming months.
